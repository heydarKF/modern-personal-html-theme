01 — the story

A short story, honestly told.

It started with a broken WordPress theme. I was seventeen, fixing a neighbor’s shop site for the price of a thank-you. The site came back online; I never did. PHP had me, and I stopped looking for the exit.

Then a client’s site got defaced. I spent a week rebuilding it and a year learning why it happened. That’s when “it works” stopped being enough — it had to be safe. Security became the lens I see every line through.

Good code reads like good prose — precise, unhurried, impossible to misread.

// the one rule I keep coming back to

Today I build pure-PHP platforms and AI agents by day, and study logic and rhetoric at the seminary by night. Two disciplines, one habit: ask better questions before writing answers. The terminal and the textbook share a desk — and, honestly, the same chair.

$ ./study --logic --rhetoric
